# Hidden logo rain

Tap the top-left LingUBible logo on Explore, Academic Tools or User Settings to trigger falling app icons. The fall starts quickly and eases out near the bottom. Tap again to restart the burst. The screen stays usable and the icons disappear automatically. Detail-page back buttons keep their normal behavior.

The Easter egg is disabled when Android system animations are disabled. It adds no saved setting, sound, vibration, permissions or network requests.

Verification and screenshots: `docs/verification/2026-10-13/icon-rain/report.md`.
